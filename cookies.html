Cookies

What this site puts on your device, and how to say no. There is not much of it, and none of it follows you anywhere.

Last updated 30 August 2026.

The short version

We use one analytics tool, Google Analytics, to see which pages people read and where they give up. That is it. There is no advertising, no retargeting, no third party selling your browsing to anyone.

Analytics runs only if you accept it. If you decline, the site works exactly the same and we simply learn less.

What gets set

NameSet byWhat it doesHow long
osc_cookie_choice This site Remembers whether you accepted or declined, so the banner stops asking Twelve months
_ga, _ga_<id> Google Analytics Tells one visit apart from another so page counts mean something. Only set if you accept Up to two years
__stripe_mid, __stripe_sid Stripe Set on the payment page only, to spot fraud. Necessary to take a payment at all Up to one year

The assessment itself keeps your answers in your own browser while you work through it, so a cancelled payment does not cost you sixty questions. That is browser storage rather than a cookie, it never leaves your device, and it clears when you close the tab.

Changing your mind

The button below clears your choice and asks again. Nothing is remembered until you answer it.

You can also block or delete cookies for every site from your browser's privacy settings, or turn on Do Not Track. Google publishes an opt-out add-on for Analytics at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Anything else

What we do with the information behind all this is set out in the privacy policy. If something here does not match what you see in your browser, tell us at jim.harvey@themessagebusiness.com and we will look at it.
