About the Pro tool

A development tool for people whose work depends on how they come across, and who want something sharper than the free version can give them.

What the Pro is

The Pro instrument is a fifty-question assessment that measures ten subscales of charisma, grouped under two dimensions: Confidence and Social Skills. It returns three scores rather than two, places you in a quadrant, gives you a banded interpretation of where each subscale sits, and identifies the two subscales most worth your attention now.

It is designed for people who already know roughly where they want to develop, and who want something more precise than a personality test. The free Open-Source Charisma Indicator places you on the map. The Pro version tells you which of ten specific skills will most repay your attention.

What the Pro is not

It is not a personality test. It does not produce a Big Five profile, a Myers-Briggs type, or any other trait-level description. People with very different personalities can produce identical Pro scores. The instrument is silent on what is going on underneath.

It is not a clinical instrument. Low scores indicate development opportunities. High scores indicate consolidated capability. Nothing here is designed for use in psychological or psychiatric diagnosis.

It is not a recruitment or selection tool. It is a self-report instrument designed for voluntary personal development. Used as a gatekeeping tool, it would produce both unfair outcomes and predictable legal exposure for anyone using it that way.

It is not a measure of moral character. The Authenticity Index measures whether your social behaviour is reliably available across audiences, which is a different question from whether you are a sincere or honest person. The instrument cannot see motive. It can see consistency.

How the Pro differs from the free version

The free Open-Source Charisma Indicator uses forty questions across four subscales (two per dimension) to place you in your charisma quadrant. It is designed as the entry point to the model: simple, fast, free.

The Pro version adds:

Ten subscales, not four

Each dimension is broken into five distinct competencies, so the score reflects which specific skill is doing the work and which is the gap.

The Authenticity Index

A third score, separate from Confidence and Social Skills, that picks up whether your behaviour is consistent across audiences or noticeably uneven.

Three-tier scoring

Each dimension is banded as Lower, Developing, or Higher. The Developing band is where most paying users sit, and where the work is most actionable.

What you actually receive

In production, the Pro generates a personalised PDF report of twelve to eighteen pages. It includes your three scores and your quadrant placement, a band-by-band reading of each subscale, two priority subscales fully expanded with specific practices, summaries of the remaining eight subscales, and references to the methods at the back of the book.

This test build does not yet generate the full report. The placeholder PDF you download at the end confirms the scoring and the technical pipeline. The real practice content is being built in the next phase of development.
