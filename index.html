The Charisma Quadrant Assessment

Sixty questions. Ten subscales. Around ten minutes. A precise reading of how you come across, whether your message lands, and where the next ten weeks of work will most repay you.

The Pro instrument measures two dimensions: Confidence and Social Skills. It places you in one of four charisma quadrants, gives you a banded interpretation of each of ten subscales, and produces two further readings the free version cannot see. An Authenticity Index that picks up the gap between how you behave with people who matter to you and everyone else. A Personal Impact score that picks up whether what you say actually lands.

The result is a personalised report of around twenty-three pages, naming the two skills where the next development work will most repay your attention, and the specific practices to start on this week.

What the report gives you

Four readings, banded in plain language. Each one tells you something different about how you land.

Confidence

Lower · Developing · Higher

How you carry yourself internally and externally. Self-worth, composure under pressure, willingness to say the difficult thing, capacity to adapt.

Social Skills

Lower · Developing · Higher

What you actually do with and for the people around you. Listening, warmth, conversational craft, emotional control.

Authenticity Index

Consistently available to Significantly instrumental

Whether your warmth and attention are reliably available across audiences, or visibly conditional on stakes, status, or usefulness.

Personal Impact

Lands reliably to Not yet landing

Whether what you say actually lands. Two components: how you build the message, and how you deliver it. Read as a multiplier on the social skills underneath.

The model in one picture

Confidence and Social Skills produce four positions. The Pro tells you which one you currently occupy, and which two of ten skills will most move you.

Who this is for

People whose work depends on the people in the room leaving it better than they came in. Anyone who already knows roughly where they want to develop, and wants something more precise than a personality test.

  • Senior managers and leaders
  • Sales professionals and account directors
  • Consultants and partners in professional services firms
  • Coaches working with their own clients
  • L&D and people-development specialists

The Pro does not test personality, intelligence, knowledge or potential. It measures the behaviours that produce the effect we call charisma in working life, and identifies where those behaviours are still uneven. If you have not yet taken the free Open-Source Charisma Indicator, that is the better place to start. You can find it at opensourcecharisma.com/assessment.

The science behind it

The Pro draws on the literature on self-awareness in leadership, metacognitive monitoring, emotional labour, authenticity in organisational psychology, and the empirical validation work the original Indicator was built on. The Methodology page sets out the construct rationale, the scoring spec, the validation status, and the references in full.
