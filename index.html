The Charisma Quadrant Assessment

Ten subscales. Fifty questions. About eight minutes. A finer reading of how you come across, and what to work on.

The Open-Source Charisma Indicator measures two dimensions: Confidence and Social Skills. The free version, taken by thousands of people, places you in one of four charisma quadrants and gives you a broad reading of where to focus.

The Pro version goes further. Ten subscales rather than four. An Authenticity Index that picks up the gap between how you behave with the people who matter to you and everyone else. A banded interpretation that tells you which two skills will most repay attention now.

This is the test build. It is hosted here for development and review. It does not yet include payment, the full practice report, or the analytics that come with the launched product. The fifty questions and the scoring are exactly as they will be in production.

What you get back

Three scores

Confidence, Social Skills, and the Authenticity Index. Each one banded as Lower, Developing, or Higher with a description of what that range means in practice.

Your quadrant type

Where you sit across the two dimensions. Courteously Charismatic, Unknowingly Influential, Occasionally Overconfident, or Incidentally Invisible.

Two priority subscales

The two skills where the work will most repay your attention now, with specific practices to start on this week.

Who this is for

The Pro instrument is built for people who already know they want to develop, and want something sharper than a generic personality test. Senior managers, sales professionals, consultants, partners in professional services firms, coaches working with their own clients. Anyone whose work depends on whether the people in the room leave it better than they came in.

It does not assess personality in the traditional sense. It does not test intelligence, knowledge, or potential. It measures the behaviours that produce the effect we call charisma in working life, and where those behaviours are still uneven.

If you have not yet taken the free Open-Source Charisma Indicator, that is the better place to start. You can find it at opensourcecharisma.com/assessment.

The science behind it

The Pro instrument draws on five lines of research: the literature on self-awareness in leadership (Eurich), metacognitive monitoring (Flavell), emotional labour (Hochschild, Grandey), authenticity in organisational psychology (Kernis, Goldman, Wood), and the empirical validation work the original Indicator was built on. The Methodology page sets out the construct rationale and the references in full.
