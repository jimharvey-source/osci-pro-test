OSCI Pro

The Charisma Quadrant Assessment

Ten subscales. Fifty questions. About eight minutes.

This is the Pro version of the Open-Source Charisma Indicator. It measures the same two dimensions as the free tool, Confidence and Social Skills, in finer detail. Ten subscales rather than four. An Authenticity Index that picks up the gap between how you behave with the people who matter to you and everyone else. A banded interpretation that tells you what to work on.

You will be asked to rate fifty statements on a five-point scale, from Strongly Disagree to Strongly Agree. There are no right answers. The instrument is reading patterns, not testing knowledge.

Answer honestly. The report you receive is only as accurate as the answers you give it.
