Why we are allowed to hold it

To give you what you bought. Your name, your answers and your order exist because you asked for a report. That is a contract, and it is the lawful basis for holding them.

To email you about this work. You gave us your address to get your report, and we also use it to send occasional emails about the Open-Source Charisma model, the book and the tools. That is our legitimate interest in staying in touch with people who have used the instrument. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and leaving the list takes one click and changes nothing about your report.

To keep the instrument honest. We look at scores across everyone who has taken the assessment to check that the cut points still sit where they should. That work uses the numbers, not the names.

To keep the accounts. HMRC requires us to keep records of what we sold and to whom.

The AI part, said plainly

Some of the writing in your report is generated rather than picked from a library. Your first name and your scores are sent to Anthropic's API, which returns two short passages of prose. The rest of the report is written by us in advance and selected by your results.

Your answers to individual questions are not sent. Your email address is not sent. The passages are checked mechanically before they reach you, and nothing you send is used to train anybody's model.

How long we keep it

Your answers and scores: two years. Long enough that you can come back for your report, and long enough to be useful for calibration. Then they are deleted.

Your order record: seven years. Not our choice. Tax records have to be kept.

Your place on the mailing list: until you leave it. One click, any time.

What you can make us do

The law gives you rights over your information. In practice they come down to this: you can ask us what we hold, ask for a copy, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, ask us to stop emailing you, and object to what we are doing with it.

Email jim.harvey@themessagebusiness.com and say which. We will do it within a month, usually a great deal sooner, and we will not ask you to justify it.

The one thing we cannot delete on request is the order record, because HMRC requires us to keep it. Everything else goes.

If we get it wrong

Tell us first, and we will try to put it right. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, or on 0303 123 1113. You do not need our permission and it does not cost anything.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so on the mailing list rather than quietly editing the page. The date at the top always tells you which version you are reading.
