Open-Source Charisma

The companion book to the OSCI Pro. The model in narrative form, with stories, characters, and ten practical chapters of practice.

About the book

The new edition of Open-Source Charisma is the companion volume to the Pro instrument. It sets out the same model the Pro measures, but in a different register. Where the Pro is the diagnostic, the book is the development pathway.

Ten chapters cover the ten subscales in turn. Each chapter combines a clear account of the underlying competency with worked examples from thirty years of practice with senior leaders, sales teams, and consultants. Real characters (renamed) doing real work, observed across multiple meetings and multiple years.

The voice is conversational rather than academic. Examples come before principles. The reader earns the framework through the stories, rather than being lectured about it up front.

What's in it

Ten chapters

One chapter per subscale. Each one starts with a working example, defines the competency, and ends with specific practices that can be applied in the next working week.

Seventeen named methods

Operational practices that cut across the chapters. The Three-Part Assertion. Strokes and Clicks. The Five-Minutes Practice. The Car-Not-Starting Principle. These are the toolkit the book teaches.

The Three Checks

The disciplines that sit underneath every method: clarity, empathy, self-awareness. A thirty-second mental check before any difficult conversation.
