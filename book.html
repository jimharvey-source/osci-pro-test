The Book

Open-Source Charisma

The companion book to the OSCI Pro. The model in narrative form, with stories, characters, and ten chapters of tried-and-tested practice for bringing your best self to every room you're in.

About the book

The new edition of Open-Source Charisma is the companion volume to the Pro instrument. The Pro is the diagnostic. The book is the development pathway. Same model, different register.

Ten chapters cover the ten subscales in turn. Each one starts with a working example, sets out the underlying competency in plain language, and ends with the practices to start on this week. The voice is conversational rather than academic. The reader earns the framework through the stories, rather than being lectured about it up front.

The examples come from thirty years of practice with senior leaders, sales teams and consultants in firms including JP Morgan, Mercer, Ford and Rolls-Royce. Real characters (renamed) doing real work, observed across multiple meetings and multiple years.

What's in it

Ten chapters, ten Steps

One chapter per subscale of the Pro instrument. Each chapter is a Step: a clear account of the underlying skill, a working example, and the practices that move the needle in the next working week.

The people in the stories

Matt, Steve, John, Mike, Samantha, and others. The book is built around the characters who taught Jim what works and what does not. The principles are earned through the stories, not asserted up front.

Tried-and-tested practice

Strategies and tactics drawn from real client work, not invented from scratch. The kind of practical, repeatable moves that help you bring your best self to every room you're in.
